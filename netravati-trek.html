About Netravati Peak Trek

Everything you need to know about this incredible Western Ghats adventure

Trek Overview

Netravati Peak stands at 1,520m in Kudremukh National Park. The trek starts from Samse village, covering 7-9km one-way through dense forests, rocky ridges, and stream crossings. Perfect for beginners with moderate fitness levels.

Nature & Wildlife

Experience rich biodiversity with chances to spot deer, monkeys, and exotic birds. The trail features beautiful two-tier waterfalls, lush greenery, and panoramic views of Kudremukh and Rani Jhari peaks from the summit.

Safety & Permits

Forest entry permits (₹500-600) and certified guides are mandatory. Trek timings: 6 AM to 6 PM only. Night trekking is strictly prohibited. Our experienced guides ensure your safety throughout the journey.

Best Time to Visit

September to February offers the best weather conditions with clear skies and pleasant temperatures. Monsoon season (June-August) is avoided due to heavy rains and slippery trails.

How to Reach

From Bangalore: 330km via Hassan-Kalasa-Samse route. We arrange jeep transportation from Samse village to the trek base camp. Homestay accommodations available in Samse and Kalasa.

What to Bring

Comfortable trekking shoes, water bottles, light snacks, rain protection, first aid basics, and camera for memories. We provide additional safety equipment and emergency supplies.
