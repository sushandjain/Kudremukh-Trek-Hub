Trail Basics

Trail Snapshot

Netravati Peak lies within the Kudremukh belt and is known for undulating meadows, breezy ridges and sweeping valley views. Ideal for beginners with decent fitness.

  • Start: Near Samse/Kalasa region (local jeep to trailhead)
  • Best Season: October – February (post‑monsoon clarity)
  • Timings: Day trek only; last exit by evening
Highlights

Why You’ll Love It

From mist‑kissed grasslands to ridge walks framed by shola forests—Netravati marries serenity with drama. On clear days, the panorama is unreal.

  • Golden meadows & cloud drifts
  • Birdlife & occasional wildlife sightings
  • Photogenic ridgelines with valley drops
Permits

Permits & Rules

  • Forest entry permit (payable at the range or via organizer)
  • Guide is mandatory (group‑wise)
  • No plastic littering; no overnight camping
Getting There

How to Reach

  • Road: Bengaluru → Hassan → Mudigere → Kalasa
  • Local jeep to the starting point (shared)
  • Plenty of homestays in Kalasa/Samse

Itinerary (Day Trek)

A well‑paced plan for comfort and views.

05:30 – 06:30

Arrive at Kalasa/Samse. Freshen up; quick breakfast. Meet guide & board jeep to trailhead.

07:15 – 09:15

13:00+

Depart for Kalasa/Bengaluru with memories (and photos!).

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries.

Is Netravati Peak suitable for beginners?
Yes. It’s a moderate day trek with steady gradients. Basic cardio fitness helps.
What should I pack?
Trekking shoes, 2L water, rainwear (seasonal), energy snacks, cap, sunscreen, personal meds, ID proof. Avoid single‑use plastic.
Are washrooms available?
Basic facilities at homestays/base. None on the trail. Plan accordingly and follow Leave No Trace.
Is camping allowed?
No. It’s restricted to day trekking with entry/exit during daylight hours.
How is mobile network?
Intermittent. Don’t rely on continuous coverage on trail. Share itinerary with family beforehand.
Can you arrange transport from Kalasa?
Yes. We coordinate jeeps to the trailhead and back as part of the Comfort or Private plans.
